_Last updated: 7 August 2026_

This page explains how to delete your **C++ IDE for Android** account and the data
stored with it. It applies to the app published by Shahid Khan under the package
name `dev.cppide.ide`.

## Before you start

If you never created an account, there is nothing to delete on our side. The app
works fully signed out, and in that mode your projects, source code, compiler
output and debugger sessions never leave your device. Uninstalling the app removes
them.

## How to request deletion

Send an email to **shahidkhan.dev88@gmail.com** from the address you signed up
with, with the subject line "Delete my C++ IDE account".

[Open a pre-filled deletion request](mailto:shahidkhan.dev88@gmail.com?subject=Delete%20my%20C%2B%2B%20IDE%20account&body=Please%20delete%20my%20C%2B%2B%20IDE%20account%20and%20all%20data%20stored%20with%20it.%0A%0AAccount%20email%3A%20)

We ask you to send it from your registered address so we can confirm the request
is really yours. If you no longer have access to that mailbox, email us anyway and
tell us your display name and roughly when you signed up, and we will work out
another way to verify you.

## What gets deleted

Everything tied to your account is removed:

- Your account record: display name, email address, user ID and password hash
- Chat messages you sent or received, including any code snapshots attached to them
- Solutions you uploaded through the app

## What is not affected

- Files stored only on your device. Those are yours and we never had a copy.
  Uninstall the app to remove them.
- Anonymous, aggregated counts that cannot be traced back to you, such as the
  total number of times an exercise pack was downloaded.

## How long it takes

We complete deletions within **30 days** of receiving your request, and email you
to confirm when it is done. In practice it is usually much faster. Deletion is
permanent, and we cannot restore an account afterwards, so please make sure you
have saved anything you want to keep first.

## Retention

We keep your account data only for as long as your account exists. There is no
separate retention period after deletion beyond the routine backup cycle, which
rolls over within 30 days.
