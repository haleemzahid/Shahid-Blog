C++ IDE ("the app") is developed by Shahid Khan. If you have any question about
this policy, contact **shahidkhan.dev88@gmail.com**.

## Using the app without an account

You can use the app without signing in. In that mode your projects, source code,
compiler output and debugger sessions stay on your device. Nothing you write is
uploaded, and the app works fully offline. Downloading exercise packs contacts our
server to fetch the exercise content, but does not require an account and does not
send us anything about you.

## What we collect if you create an account

When you sign up or sign in we collect and store:

- **Your display name, email address and password.** These are sent to our own
  server to create and authenticate your account. We do not use a third-party
  sign-in provider.
- **Chat messages you send.** The app includes a chat where you can discuss an
  exercise with a teacher. Messages can carry a snapshot of the code file you were
  working on at the time, so the person helping you can see it. If you do not want
  a piece of code shared, do not send a message from that file.
- **Solutions you upload.** When you use the upload feature, the source code of
  your solution is sent to our server and stored against your account so you can
  retrieve it later and so a teacher can review it.

## How your login is stored on your device

If you tick "Remember me", your session token is saved in the app's private
storage so you stay signed in. If you do not tick it, the token is kept only in
memory and is gone when the app closes. Signing out removes the saved token.

## What we do not do

- We do not sell or share your personal data with third parties.
- We include no third-party advertising or analytics SDKs.
- We do not request or collect your location, contacts, camera, microphone, call
  logs, or any files outside the app's own storage.
- We do not upload your code except in the two cases named above: a code snapshot
  attached to a chat message, and a solution you choose to upload.

## Where your data goes

Account details, chat messages and uploaded solutions are stored on servers we
operate. All requests are made over HTTPS.

## How long we keep it, and how to delete it

We keep your account data for as long as your account exists. To delete your
account and everything stored with it — chat messages and uploaded solutions
included — email **shahidkhan.dev88@gmail.com** from the address you signed up
with. We will delete it within 30 days and confirm when it is done.

## Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect
personal information from them. If you believe a child has created an account,
contact us and we will remove it.

## Security

We use HTTPS for all network requests. No system is perfectly secure, and we
cannot guarantee absolute security.

If this policy changes we will update this page and change the date at the top.
